Ren

Hello and welcome, I'm Ren. First, a little about me personally. I'm originally from NYC and proudly so. In the city that never sleeps, I love spending time with friends over drinks, playing board/video games, or eating out. I've been a techie for as long as I can remember, starting by exploring the functionality of the family computer, to building my own, to branching out into all kinds of home-lab pet-projects. For days when I prefer some quiet time, I love to read while listening along to the audiobook version. I have a thirst for knowledge and innate curiosity that drives me to keep going until I've accomplished what I've set out to do.

Professionally, I began in the IT field, working at Goldman Sachs. After many years of it, I wanted to see the world and try something new, so now I find myself an English teacher in China. I've taught for an independent workshop and most recently at a more traditional training center, Yingfu. I've taught foundational English to young kids from Pre-K to middle school and IELTS level English to high school teens and adults. My ability to be adaptable and flexible has served me well as I've transitioned careers, and is indispensable in a field as full of surprises as teaching. Teaching has proven to be extremely fulfilling and I hope to continue to improve and help as many students achieve their goals as I can.
