Module 1 (ED 501)

The video on Edpuzzle gave information on where to find many useful documents and resources, such as the candidate handbook. The handbook acts as a roadmap for the course, outlining the academic standards of the course, the target GPA needed to successfully complete the course, assists in planning ahead for the clinical practice, among other things.

By using the 10 InTASC Standards and the Clinical Rubric together, they clearly illustrate what effective teaching looks like across the 4 core domains of The Learner and Learning, Content, Instructional Practice, and Professional Responsibility. The clinical rubric translates the 10 standards into specific guiding questions with which to judge and score a student's work performance. It is useful as a resource for us to see what areas we are lacking in and can improve at.
